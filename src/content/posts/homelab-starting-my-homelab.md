I've been wanting to start a homelab/home server for a long time now.

Use cases I envision:

- Self-hosting Home Assistant for smart home automations across multiple ecosystems
- Access to smart home appliances when away:
  - Did I leave the porch door open when I left the house?
  - Was there a leak in the basement (which has happened in the past)?
- Self-hosting applications for personal use:
  - Immich for photo management
  - paperless-ngx for document management
  - ...TBD
- Gaining experience:
  - with networking (DNS, virtual networks, reverse proxies, etc.)
  - with Proxmox
- Fun and experimentation!
